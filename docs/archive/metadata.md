# Titles, descriptions, hashtags

## Title

The title is a second hook aimed at browsers rather than scrollers. Same rules as
`hooks.md`, with one addition: include a **searchable proper noun** — a person, company,
place, or product — that the on-screen overlay may have left implicit.

- Front-load the specific part. Truncation on mobile is aggressive.
- One idea. A title carrying two claims delivers neither.
- Do not stack hashtags into the title; they push out the words doing the work.
- Match the language of the audience, not the creator's convenience. A Georgian clip
  gets a Georgian title even if the channel occasionally posts in English.

**Good:** `Moscow couldn't dictate to NASA — here's Carl Sagan's answer`
Conflict, a named authority, and an answer you have to watch to hear.

**Weak:** `AI is advancing — and we're standing still`
Position statement. No name, no number, nothing at stake, and it would fit any channel.

## Description

Shorts descriptions are barely read but still indexed. Keep it short and useful:

```
<one sentence of context the title could not fit>

<source>: <long-form video title> — <link>
<3–5 hashtags>
```

Linking the source long-form video is the point. Shorts viewers are the main funnel into
the long-form catalogue, and that funnel does not exist unless the link does.

## Hashtags

3–5, placed in the description. More dilutes and looks like spam.

Mix scopes: one broad (`#shorts`), one topical (`#nasa`), one niche or local
(`#ქართული`). All-broad tags compete with everything; all-niche tags reach nobody.

Reuse a stable set per topic bucket so the channel's clips cluster in recommendations
rather than each arriving as an orphan.

## Thumbnail

Shorts mostly play from the feed and the custom thumbnail matters less than for
long-form — but it is what appears on the channel's Shorts tab, which viewers do browse
after a clip lands.

Pick a frame with a clear face and visible expression. Avoid frames where the caption
overlay covers the face.

## Pre-publish check

- [ ] Plays correctly at 1080×1920, audio in sync
- [ ] Captions readable and not colliding with the Shorts UI (bottom ~580 px)
- [ ] Hook overlay fully inside the frame, no clipped text
- [ ] No `>>` or `[bracketed tags]` leaking into captions
- [ ] Title carries a searchable noun
- [ ] Description links the source long-form video
- [ ] Not a duplicate of an existing Short
- [ ] **The creator has seen it and said yes**

The last item is not a formality. Render, show, wait.
