02 · YouTube Studio CSV
Studio → Analytics → Advanced mode → per-video report → Export CSV. Use English column names and decimal dots. Import is local. No Google sign-in or OAuth is implemented in this version.
Supported metrics: Views, Impressions, Impressions click-through rate (%), Watch time (hours), Average view duration, Subscribers. Missing values stay unknown. A retention curve cannot be inferred from average watch time.


03 · Privacy & controls
No browsing history, cookies or background channel crawling. Only the open YouTube page and your selected CSV are used. AI sends a capped text context and imported metrics to your chosen bridge/provider only after you click Send. Thumbnail pixels are sent only when you explicitly enable image analysis for a video; a vision-capable model is required. No video title, thumbnail or publishing setting is changed.
ChatGPT/Codex vidIQ authorization is separate and is not copied into this extension.
